Our Process

The 6-Step ToyNest Hygiene Protocol

Every returned toy goes through this complete process before being approved for the next family.

1

Visual Inspection & Logging

Every returned toy is photographically documented and visually inspected for damage, missing parts, or wear. This creates an audit trail for each toy in our system.

2

Pre-Wash & Debris Removal

Toys are pre-washed with warm water to remove surface debris and loose contamination. Small parts and fabric components are separated for specialized cleaning.

3

Hospital-Grade Sanitization

All toys are treated with WHO-approved, non-toxic, child-safe disinfectants. Our sanitizers eliminate 99.9% of bacteria, viruses, and pathogens without leaving harmful residues.

4

UV-C Sterilization Chamber

After washing, toys undergo UV-C light sterilization in our professional-grade sterilization chamber. UV-C light destroys microorganisms that sanitizers may miss, particularly in crevices.

5

Safety & Completeness Inspection

A trained QC specialist inspects each toy for broken parts, sharp edges, missing pieces, or any safety hazard. Any toy that fails this inspection is immediately retired.

6

Eco-Seal & Certification Sticker

Approved toys receive our tamper-evident hygiene seal and a dated certification sticker. This seal confirms the toy has passed all 5 previous steps and is ready for a new family.
