Sign Up & Personalize

Create Your Family Profile

Tell us about your child — their age, interests, and what kind of play they love. Do they prefer outdoor adventures? Building with blocks? Creative arts? Our smart profiling system uses this to curate the perfect toy selection for your family.

You can set toy preferences, age ranges, and even blacklist specific categories. Your profile is always editable from the parent dashboard.

2
Plan Selection

Choose Your Subscription Plan

Pick the plan that fits your family's needs. Our Sprout plan is ideal for babies (0–2 years), Explorer is perfect for toddlers (2–6 years), and Adventure is designed for school-age kids (6–12 years).

All plans include free delivery, free returns, and our hygiene guarantee. Cancel or pause anytime with no penalty.

3
Receive & Play

Unbox the Joy & Play All Month

Your curated toy box arrives within 48 hours, eco-packed and ready for exploration. Each box includes an activity guide with age-appropriate play ideas and developmental tips to help you get the most out of every toy.

Every toy has been professionally cleaned, inspected for safety, and verified to be in excellent condition before reaching your door.

4
Return & Rotate

Return Easily, Receive Freshness

At the end of your subscription cycle, we send a prepaid return bag directly to your home. Simply pack all the toys (no cleaning needed!), and schedule a pickup at your convenience. Within days, a brand-new selection arrives.

Use the parent dashboard to request early swaps if your child has finished exploring a toy before the month ends.
